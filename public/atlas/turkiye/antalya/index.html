Antalya Architecture & Travel Guide

Beyond the beach: a compact old town, layered gateways and remarkable ancient cities along the coast.

01 / ORIENTATION

Read the city in layers.

Start within Kaleiçi, Antalya’s historic centre. Its street pattern, courtyard houses and harbour setting bring different periods into close conversation. Hadrian’s Gate marks a Roman threshold; the Yivli Minare ensemble speaks to the Seljuk city. Perge and Aspendos are separate regional excursions, not stops on the same short urban walk.

Use this guide as an architectural itinerary rather than a live booking service. Confirm access, opening hours and transport with the relevant venue before departure.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

Five places to study.

Within the city

  1. Hadrian’s Gate: examine the Roman triumphal gateway and its relationship to today’s street.
  2. Kaleiçi lanes: look for overhanging upper floors, shaded passages and courtyard thresholds.
  3. Yivli Minare: study the distinctive fluted minaret and Seljuk urban layer.

Beyond the centre

  1. Perge: trace the ancient street and civic plan on a dedicated visit.
  2. Aspendos: focus on the scale and acoustical form of the Roman theatre.

Ancient sites require separate travel; do not assume both can be covered comfortably on foot or during a short city walk.

03 / ARCHITECTURE ROUTES

Two ways to explore.

Kaleiçi walking loop

Begin at Hadrian’s Gate, follow the old-town lanes toward Yivli Minare, then descend toward the historic harbour. Allow around two hours for an unhurried exterior-focused walk, more with interiors or stops. Terrain includes uneven paving and slopes.

Ancient-city study day

Plan Perge and Aspendos as a separate excursion. Compare the organisation of a Roman urban site with a theatre conceived for collective spectacle. Check site conditions, transport and opening times in advance.

04 / PRACTICAL NOTES

Stay, eat, move.

Where to base yourself

Kaleiçi keeps the historic walk close at hand; properties around the old town vary in accessibility and vehicle access. Areas beyond the core may make onward transport simpler. Check actual property access rather than relying on a neighbourhood label.

Food and pacing

Use a meal break in or near the old town to reset between the harbour and the upper streets. Choose independently verified venues; this guide does not recommend unreviewed businesses or claim current prices.
