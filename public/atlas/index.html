HOW TO USE THE ATLAS

Start with one city, walk it in half-day routes.

Each guide is built the same way: a curated edit of the buildings that explain the city, half-day walking routes that connect them, and practical notes on timing, tickets and viewpoints. The Türkiye series begins with Istanbul, continues along the coast to Antalya and ends among the rock-cut settlements of Cappadocia; the Gulf series opens with Dubai and the Caucasus series opens with Baku and continues to Tbilisi. Every guide can also be downloaded as a short PDF to keep offline. For the ideas behind the routes, read the architecture magazine and the daily briefing.
