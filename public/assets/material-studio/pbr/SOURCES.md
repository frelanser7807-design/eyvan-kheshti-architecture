Material Studio PBR texture sources

The bundled 1K JPG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps are local copies of these Poly Haven assets. Each asset page states that it is CC0.

- White Plaster 02 — author: Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02
- Wood Floor — author: Dimitrios Savva — https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_floor
- Floor Tiles 02 — author: Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/floor_tiles_02
- Concrete Floor Worn 001 — authors: Dimitrios Savva (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing) — https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001

Each PBR material uses its diffuse map for color, nor_gl map for surface relief, and rough map for light response. The page labels the preview as a concept visualisation, not a construction specification.
