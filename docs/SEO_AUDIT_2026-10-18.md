Google-Extended governs Gemini training and some grounding, not Google Search eligibility. Cloudflare dashboard requires authentication to inspect/change effective Search/Agent/Training policy; do not assume changing repository robots.txt overrides edge blocking. Keep training preferences distinct from search/agent retrieval. Existing llms.txt is an optional discovery guide, not an indexing protocol.
